Straight answers

FAQ

Will AI post things without me knowing?

No. Every post sits in your approval queue until you say yes, and nothing goes live without it.

Can someone hack it by messaging my page?

Messages and comments are treated as information, never as commands. Nothing is 100% guaranteed, which is why your approval and the audit trail are always there as the last line.

Why do I pay for discovery?

It's a real piece of work. You get a written plan and a fixed quote, and the fee is credited to your setup if you go ahead within 30 days.

What does "usage at cost" mean?

AI tools and software charge per use. Ideally those accounts are in your name with a spending cap; if we ever pay one for you, it's passed on at exactly what it cost, with no markup, line by line in your report.

Is this only for social media?

No. AgentOS also runs WhatsApp enquiry helpers, booking and quote follow-ups, internal checklists and weekly reports, all on one dashboard with the same approval locks.

Do you run paid ads?

No. We focus on organic growth: good content, consistently.

Do I own my content?

Yes. Your posts, footage and accounts are yours.

Do you charge VAT?

No VAT charged (not VAT-registered). No VAT is added to our prices.
